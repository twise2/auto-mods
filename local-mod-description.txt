Exploding Kings: Kings explode on death in a chain reaction, and can garrison siege weapons or be converted like any other unit. Rewarding Snipes: when a King dies it leaves behind a permanent, always-visible relic cart worth 50 bonus population, so killing an enemy King is a real, lasting reward for whoever secures the kill. Heroes & Villains: every civilization gets a hero unit, a land hero trainable at the Castle and, for civs with a strong naval history, a water hero trainable at the Dock. Heroes cost food and gold and are limited to one at a time. Each hero grants a passive aura based on its type - Archer/Infantry grant move speed and attack speed, Cavalry grant move speed, Warships/Cavalry Archers grant attack speed, and Monks grant healing - drawn from existing unit stats for balance. Civilization Identity Expansion: dozens of regional and historical unit/building grants giving civs more of what they'd plausibly have fielded historically, reusing only units and buildings that already exist in the game. Steppe Lancer, Elephant Archer, Armored Elephant, and Genitour extended to civs with matching cavalry heritage. Camel Scout given as an early option to camel-heritage civs, with Berbers, Saracens, Hindustanis, and Ethiopians now actually starting the game scouting with a Camel Scout instead of a Scout Cavalry. Winged Hussars, Legionary, Condottiero, and Thirisadai extended to fitting civs. Missionaries and Warrior Priests extended to more civs with a matching religious tradition. Settlement extended to Aztecs, Mayans, and Incas, replacing Mill/Lumber Camp/Mining Camp from early game; Folwark extended to Bohemians and Lithuanians the same way. Mule Carts extended to more nomadic civs. Fire Lancer, Rocket Cart, Traction Trebuchet, and Lou Chuan extended across East Asian gunpowder civs. Hei-Kuang Cavalry given to Chinese as a Knight-line replacement; Grenadier given to Chinese, Khitans, Vietnamese, Mongols, Koreans, and Turks in place of Hand Cannoneer. Jian Swordsman extended to Shu and Wei; Temple Guard extended to Incas and Aztecs. Knight/Cavalier/Paladin access removed, both the units and the research, for civs whose real identity replaces it - Turks, Berbers, Saracens, Malay, Burmese, Khmer, Vietnamese, and Chinese. Cosmetic skins: Frankish Paladin for Franks/French, Crusader Knight for Crusader states. Samurai gained a situational ranged-attack swap; Longboats can now transport units. Regional buildings extended: Feitoria, Folwark, Donjon, Krepost, Harbor, Fortified Church. Community Games balance changes: all towers - Watch Tower, Guard Tower, Keep, and Bombard Tower - now cost 1 population each. Trade Caravan research is significantly more expensive (800 food, 200 gold).
